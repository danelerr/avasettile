Avalanche C-Chain Round 4
Round Details
Overview

Retro9000 supports ecosystem growth and innovation that reinforces network utility and adoption. Round 4 provides retroactive funding to teams building on the Avalanche C-Chain that generate real on-chain activity measured by AVAX burned through transaction fees.

Round 4 introduces two major updates to the program: a verified user tier system where community verification directly impacts project scores, and a reward function that gives smaller projects a larger share of rewards.

All projects from Round 1, 2 and 3 are automatically entered into Round 4 after they complete KYC/KYB and accept the new Terms and Conditions.

Eligibility

Avalanche C-Chain projects: contracts must be deployed on Avalanche C-Chain mainnet and generate activity that burns AVAX.
KYC/KYB Verification: all projects must complete and satisfy KYC/KYB verification before rewards may be claimed. Verification is valid for one year.
Terms and Conditions: all projects, including returning projects from Round 1, 2 and 3, must accept the updated Terms and Conditions. Acceptance is required to continue participating in subsequent rounds and claim rewards.
Contract ownership: projects must prove ownership with a deployer signature or sufficient supporting evidence.
Contract verification: contracts must be verified on Snowtrace or Avascan. Unverified contracts are ineligible.
Funding considerations: other Foundation funding may be considered. Projects that received grants based on gas burned or number of transactions on the Avalanche C-Chain are not eligible.
Projects must be approved for Retro9000 and listed on the Discover Projects page. The Foundation periodically reviews eligibility.
Meeting eligibility requirements does not guarantee selection or disbursement. All funding decisions are at the sole discretion of the Avalanche Foundation.
Timeline

Each round lasts the duration of a calendar month. Round 4 runs throughout June 2026.

We encourage projects to apply early to maximise visibility and community support.

Monthly project snapshots will capture activity across the full round period, including time before a project's signup if it joins late.

User Tiers

Round 4 introduces a three-tier user system that directly influences project scores. Projects are incentivised to bring their communities onto the platform and encourage them to verify.

Unregistered - users who interact with a project onchain but have not yet connected to the Retro9000 platform. Unregistered users contribute the least to a project's score.
Connected - users who have connected their wallet to the Retro9000 platform and have sent at least one transaction on Avalanche within the past 30 days.
Verified - users who have connected their wallet and linked their X (Twitter) account. X accounts are reviewed against eligibility criteria including account age, follower count, and posting history to maintain the integrity of the program. Verified users contribute the most to a project's score.
A project's score is directly influenced by the proportion of verified users in its active user base. Projects with a higher proportion of verified users will score stronger than those with largely unregistered users, even with similar levels of AVAX burned.

Evaluation

Projects are evaluated on the generation of organic, real on-chain activity measured by points based on AVAX burned and verified user engagement. Points track AVAX burned except where a multiplier or user tier weighting applies.

Transaction count methodology: only direct, first-level calls to a contract are counted to prevent double counting. Downstream gas delegated to aggregators is excluded from the calculation.

Round 4 Multipliers

Build Games tag: projects that reached Stage 2: MVP of Build Games receive a 5x leaderboard ranking multiplier for this round.
New project tag: projects joining the ecosystem for the first time receive a 5x leaderboard ranking multiplier. To qualify, project contracts must have been deployed within two months of the beginning of the current round, and the project must not have received a new project multiplier in past rounds.
Rewards

Top 20 projects on the leaderboard at round end are considered for disbursements.
Rewards are distributed according to the power-weighted share of individual project’s scores that gives smaller projects a meaningfully larger share relative to what they have burned.
Rewarded funds must be reinvested into the project as per the Terms and Conditions.
All rewards will be disbursed via airdrop following successful KYC/KYB verification.
Previous funding provided by the Avalanche Foundation may be taken into account when considering rewards.
No single project can claim more than 25% of the total prize pool for the round.
$200K lifetime cap per project across rounds. After exceeding this cap, the project may no longer participate in the program.
Minimum reward of 10 AVAX required to be eligible for disbursement
Reward Pool

The aggregate reward pool for Avalanche C-Chain Round 4 will be a total of up to 10,000 AVAX or the total AVAX burned during the round, whichever is less. Future round reward allocation may vary at the discretion of the Avalanche Foundation.

Referrals

Referrers can earn up to $3,000 in AVAX per project, per round if the project goes on to receive funding. Visit retro9000.avax.network, connect your wallet, and claim your referral link.
